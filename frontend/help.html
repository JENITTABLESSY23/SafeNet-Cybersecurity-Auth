AccessHow do I reset my password?
Click Sign in on the login page, then select Forgot password? Enter your registered hospital email and you'll receive a reset link within 5 minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact IT support.
AccessWhat is my Hospital ID format?
Your Hospital ID follows the format SN-XXXX-0000 — two letters for your department prefix, four letters for your name code, and a four-digit number. Example: SN-AM07-2024. You can find it on your welcome email or by asking your department admin.
AccessHow do I set up two-factor authentication?
Go to Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication. Toggle on the Authenticator App (TOTP) option and scan the QR code with Google Authenticator or Authy. You'll be asked to enter a 6-digit verification code to confirm setup.
PatientsHow do I add a new patient?
Navigate to Patient Records from the sidebar, then click Add Patient in the top-right corner. Fill in the patient's name, age, gender, department, bed number, and diagnosis, then click Save Patient. The record will appear in the table immediately.
PatientsHow do I discharge or remove a patient?
In the Patient Records table, click Edit on the patient row and change the status to Discharged to keep the record archived, or click Delete to remove the record permanently. Note: deletions cannot be undone — confirm before proceeding.
IoTWhat does an anomaly alert mean?
SafeNet uses an Isolation Forest algorithm to continuously analyse IoT telemetry. When a reading falls outside the expected cluster boundaries, it raises a WARNING (sensor drift / device malfunction) or CRITICAL (security / access violation) alert. These appear in the Admin panel's anomaly log and require triage.
IoTHow do I handle a sensor drift warning?
A Sensor Drift classification usually indicates the monitoring device needs recalibration or replacement. First verify the patient's condition manually. If the patient is stable, submit an IoT maintenance ticket via the Help desk. If critical, use manual monitoring and notify the biomedical team immediately.
ICUWhen should I use Break the Glass?
Break the Glass (BTG) is an emergency RBAC override for urgent clinical scenarios where standard access controls would delay life-critical care. It must only be used in genuine emergencies — a clinical reason is required on activation. All BTG events are permanently logged in the SafeNet audit trail and reviewed by your supervisor.
AdminHow long does staff registration approval take?
New registrations are reviewed by the Admin team within 1–2 business days. You'll receive an email to your registered address once your account is activated. If you haven't heard after 2 days, contact IT support with your submitted Hospital ID proof reference number.
AdminHow do I request a role change?
RBAC roles are managed by the Admin team. To request a role change (e.g. department transfer or promotion), submit a support ticket below or email it-support@hospital.in with your Hospital ID, current role, and the requested new role. Your department head will need to co-sign the request.
SecurityHow do I sign out of other devices?
Go to Settings → Security → Active Sessions. You'll see all devices currently signed in. Click Revoke next to any session you don't recognise, or click Sign out all other sessions to immediately invalidate all devices except your current one.
SecurityIs SafeNet HIPAA compliant?
Yes. SafeNet is built to HIPAA, ISO 27001, and DPDP Act standards. All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Access is controlled by RBAC, all actions are logged in an immutable audit trail, and the system undergoes quarterly third-party security audits.
